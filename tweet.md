weekend hack! biscotti is an open, privacy-first, Instinct-like iMessage agent. it's minimal (a ~650-line core!) and extensible, largely reliant on off-the-shelf infra (Pi, Firecracker VMs, Nix, Tailscale) and light primitives for memory, secrets, and crons.

i'm running it on a Raspberry Pi but it works anywhere
