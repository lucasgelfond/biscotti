# Recording the demos

Real biscotti, a real iOS screen: the owner's iPhone through iPhone Mirroring, driven from the Mac (scripted clicks and
typing into the mirroring window), recorded with `screencapture -v` (whole screen). Warp needs Screen Recording and
Accessibility permissions. The Mac is tied up while recording.

## Before every run

- Clean slate: clear the biscotti conversation (its transcript, and the Messages thread on the phone) before the whole
  run, and before each separate run.
- Memories to set up first:
  - lives at 710 Degraw Street in Brooklyn, but goes to Manhattan often
  - parents are in Los Angeles; grew up there
  - prefers window seats, and to leave in the morning / not get in super late
- The card is vaulted before recording (through the vault form, never in chat).
- Only one biscotti on Photon; don't run `notify.ts` while it's live.

## The texts, in order

1. when did my landlords last fix the AC?
2. can you look at JFK to SFO flights around nov 14?
3. can you book the [----]? (a flight from biscotti's answer)
4. when is the earliest i can see the cameron winter concert film?
5. can you look for three seats together at angelika?
6. remind me to ask my parents about thanksgiving

Flights should take a few turns: the first answer gives times quickly, then biscotti looks at the seat maps.

Typing: mostly correct but a bit irregular, like a fast (not impossibly fast) typist, about 95% accuracy (the odd typo,
sometimes fixed).

## Outputs (saved as site artifacts for review)

- One video for Twitter with all four together: landlord/AC, flights (several turns), the reminder, Cameron Winter.
- Clean separate runs of the three tasks (landlord/AC, flights, Cameron Winter), each recorded on its own.
- GIFs of the three tasks (not the reminder) for the site's Examples section, cutting most of the waiting but keeping
  the typing bubbles.
